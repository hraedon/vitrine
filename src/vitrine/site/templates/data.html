How to cite

For a number, cite the primary source named by its source in JSON (or source_id in CSV), then cite the vitrine corpus for the compilation and tier assignment. The repository's CITATION.cff is the corpus citation; the exporter software is separately MIT-licensed. The corpus and rendered site are licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

This export contains aggregate facts and citations only; underlying source material keeps the terms stated on its source record. The full series observations included in corpus.json make computed derivations reproducible without republishing raw source files.
