# Japan as a focused exhibit

The visitor entry point is `collections/japan.html`; all seven decade folders
remain available, with their authored observations, gaps, record IDs and source
cards unchanged. The exhibit selects questions rather than pretending each
decade can furnish a complete household.

## Editorial status

`site/curation/collections.py` records research holding, focused exhibit and
guided room choices with rationales. New rooms default to research holdings,
regardless of country or record count. A guided room must resolve its curated
opening route. Status describes presentation, not evidence quality or an audit
result. Existing source tiers continue to describe the cited evidence.

The Japan exhibit is focused; its decade folders remain research holdings.
The early US rooms with substantial structural gaps are explicitly research
or focused presentations. The US comparative registries remain independent:
Japan's exhibit does not enroll Japan in decade-keyed US charts.

## Selection and boundaries

- Household budgets separate workers' household income, all-household
  consumption, and food's share of consumption. The FIES urban/national
  coverage seam is visible. No income-minus-consumption inference is offered.
- Workplace earnings and hours remain distinct from household accounts.
  Early yearbook observations and later MLS records have separate source
  lenses. Nominal pay does not establish purchasing power.
- Home and equipment selections distinguish dwelling space, television
  categories, durable-goods ownership and communications penetration. Source
  population and the record's unit appear beside each value.

Selection declarations hold fact IDs and framing, never new numeric values.
The projection requires one observation year in each fact's label, consistent
with its price year where present. It never substitutes the archive decade or
source publication year. Thus the NSFIIE observations from 2009 explicitly
retain that date while linking back to their existing 2010s folder.

The first observation in each source lens is visible immediately; additional
dated observations use native disclosures. All source cards and decade links
work without JavaScript. Different observations are not joined into a trend
line: comparability requires additional source qualification, not just a shared
currency, subject or neighboring year.

## Presentation pilot

The US 1950s room leads its object gallery with the television record, giving
the dated ownership observation visual emphasis and keeping the other objects
accessible. Its opening question asks who was measured. Illustrations identify
subjects, not exact models or evidence that every family owned them.

Validation covers source membership, duplicate and missing selections, gaps
misclassified as observations, ambiguous dates, retained record links,
population visibility, mobile layout and source cards without JavaScript.
